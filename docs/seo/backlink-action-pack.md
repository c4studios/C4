# C4 Studios — Free Authority Backlink & Citation Action Pack

> Source: research pass, 14 July 2026. Business-action checklist (nothing here is code). Every source was checked live or corroborated via search; `rel` (dofollow/nofollow) labels are best-effort norms — verify after you publish each profile.

## Read this first — the honest reality

1. **Most of these links are `nofollow`, and that's fine.** Directory/profile links from Google, Yellow Pages, LinkedIn, Crunchbase etc. are almost all `nofollow`. Their value isn't classic "link juice" — it's **entity + citation value**: consistent Name / Area / Email / Website (NAP) across trusted domains is what Google's entity graph and AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini) read to decide C4 Studios is a real, specific business. That *is* "the backlinks AI search loves," but the mechanism is trust/consistency, not PageRank.

2. **The government supplier/tender portals do NOT give you a public website backlink.** This is the myth to kill. AusTender, Tenders WA and VendorPanel registrations create a **private account** for tender alerts and bidding — there is no public, crawlable supplier profile linking your site. Register them for genuine business development and entity/trust value, not as "free gov backlinks." ICN Gateway can show a public profile, but only on a **paid** tier.

**The single biggest lever is NAP consistency** — use the paste-ready block at the bottom verbatim, everywhere.

> **One decision before you start:** lock in **one phone number** and decide address vs service-area business (SAB), then keep them identical across every profile. Inconsistent phone/address is the #1 thing that dilutes this whole exercise.

## Tier 1 — Do these first (free, public, real website link, highest trust)

### 1. Google Business Profile
- **URL:** https://business.google.com/aunz/business-profile/
- **Authority:** highest possible; feeds Google Maps, the local pack, and Google AI Overviews.
- **Requires:** Google account, verification (video/phone/postcard). No ABN. Free.
- **Public + link:** Yes — prominent website link, drives the Maps local pack.
- **Effort:** 30–45 min + verification wait. **Non-negotiable — start here.** Set up as a **service-area business** (hide address, list Perth/Fremantle/WA suburbs).

### 2. Bing Places for Business
- **URL:** https://www.bing.com/forbusiness/
- **Authority:** high; powers Bing Maps **and is a known data source for ChatGPT/Copilot** — punches above its search share for AI visibility.
- **Requires:** Microsoft account; **import directly from Google Business Profile** in a couple of clicks. Free.
- **Effort:** ~10 min if you import from GBP.

### 3. Apple Business Connect
- **URL:** https://businessconnect.apple.com/
- **Authority:** highest trust; controls Apple Maps, Siri, Spotlight, Wallet. High iPhone penetration in AU makes this meaningful. Free.
- **Effort:** 20–30 min + verification.

### 4. LinkedIn Company Page
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/
- **Authority:** very high; strongly weighted in entity/knowledge-panel signals and frequently cited by AI answer engines.
- **Link type:** `nofollow`, but a top-tier entity anchor.
- **Effort:** ~20 min. Add the ~50-word description, logo, Perth location.

## Tier 2 — High-authority business/industry profiles (free tier, directly relevant)

### 5. Clutch (free "Basic" profile)
- **URL:** https://clutch.co/get-listed — leading directory for web/dev/AI/creative agencies. Free Basic tier (Verified is $499/yr — **not** needed).
- **Link:** carries a link to your site (verify current `rel` after publishing). High-relevance authority + strong AI-search citation.
- **Effort:** 30–45 min; add 1–2 client references to unlock reviews.

### 6. Crunchbase (free company profile)
- **URL:** https://www.crunchbase.com/ → Create Profile. A **primary structured-data source for AI models** and Google's entity graph — excellent for "who is C4 Studios" answers.
- **Link:** typically `nofollow`; premium entity/citation value.
- **Effort:** 20–30 min.

### 7. GoodFirms (free listing)
- **URL:** https://www.goodfirms.co/list-your-company (open in a browser — blocks bots). High-authority B2B/IT services review directory.
- **Effort:** ~30 min.

### 8. DesignRush (free basic listing)
- **URL:** https://www.designrush.com/agency — high-authority agency directory (web/branding/video). Free basic listing worthwhile; **skip the paid $2.4k–3.6k/yr tiers.**
- **Effort:** ~20 min (lower priority than Clutch + GoodFirms).

*Optional same-category extras (free, lower priority):* The Manifest (themanifest.com — often auto-created from Clutch), Sortlist (sortlist.com).

## Tier 3 — Reputable Australian consumer directories (free, mostly `nofollow`)

### 9. Yellow Pages Australia ⭐ (one action = three listings)
- **URL:** https://www.yellow.com.au/online-signup — free basic listing **auto-syndicates to True Local and Whereis** (all Thryv-managed). One action, three AU directories. Paid tiers not needed.
- **Effort:** ~20 min. **Best-value single AU directory action.**

### 10. Hotfrog Australia
- **URL:** https://www.hotfrog.com.au/ → "Add your business" (open in browser).
- **Link:** historically **`dofollow`** on free listings — one of the few here that may pass link equity. **Verify `rel` after publishing.**
- **Effort:** ~15 min.

### 11. Yelp Australia
- **URL:** https://biz.yelp.com/ — feeds Apple Maps and various AI sources. `nofollow`; strong citation.
- **Effort:** ~15 min.

*Optional reputable AU extras (free, ~10 min each — pick a couple, don't spam):* StartLocal, Localsearch, dLook, AussieWeb. Also a **Facebook Business Page** (facebook.com/pages/create) as a baseline public entity profile.

## Tier 4 — Government & procurement (register for trust + business dev — NOT free backlinks)

None of these give a public crawlable profile with a website link. Register for real WA/federal contract access and because being named on a public **contract notice** is a genuine `.gov.au` **entity citation** (no link).

- **12. ABN Lookup** — https://abr.business.gov.au/ — your ABN record is already public; ensure it's current and the entity name matches your NAP exactly. The canonical "C4 Studios is a real registered business" anchor. No link.
- **13. AusTender (Federal)** — https://www.tenders.gov.au/RegisteredUser/Register — free; private account for alerts/bidding. No public profile/link. Winning work → public Contract Notice names you (citation, no link).
- **14. Tenders WA (State)** ⭐ — https://www.wa.gov.au/service/trade/trade-development/register-your-business-supplier-tenders-wa — free; most relevant procurement portal for a Perth studio. No public profile/link.
- **15. VendorPanel** — https://www.vendorpanel.com.au/ — free supplier account for council/agency RFQs (lighter WA adoption — check which Perth-metro councils use it). No public profile/link.
- **16. ICN Gateway** — https://gateway.icn.org.au/join — ⚠️ free tier does **not** display your profile publicly; public profile starts at paid "Be Seen" (~$190/yr). **Skip as a "free backlink"**; only pay if targeting major-project (mining/construction/infrastructure) supply chains.

## Tier 5 — Local council / regional (check fit — most don't fit a B2B studio)

- **17. Visit Perth business directory** — https://visitperth.com/business-directory — tourism/retail-oriented (`.com`, not `.gov.au`). Only if you have a CBD presence; verify eligibility + whether listings link out.
- **18. Your own council's business directory** — many Perth councils added "support/shop local" directories post-COVID. If yours is free and links out, a real council citation is high-value. Verify per council.
- **19. CCIWA** — https://cciwa.com/membership/ — high-authority WA body, but member directory needs **paid membership.** Join for network/advocacy, not as a free link.

## Considered and REJECTED (don't waste time / avoid risk)

- **"Top 250 / 60 free Australian listing sites" mass-submission packs** — low quality, shared spam footprint that can *hurt*. ~15 curated sources beat 250 junk ones.
- **cityofperth.net** — a private directory with a council-sounding name; **not** the City of Perth (that's perth.wa.gov.au / visitperth.com).
- **Paid tiers** (DesignRush/ICN paid, Clutch Verified $499) — business decisions, not free links.
- **"Buy 500 backlinks" / PBN / Fiverr packages** — manual-penalty risk. Avoid entirely.
- **Perth, *Ontario* directories** (perthcounty.ca, westperth.com) — wrong hemisphere. Ignore.

## Recommended order of execution

1. **Week 1 (core entity):** Google Business Profile → Bing Places (import from Google) → Apple Business Connect → LinkedIn Company Page.
2. **Week 1–2 (authority/relevance):** Clutch (free) → Crunchbase → GoodFirms.
3. **Week 2 (AU directories):** Yellow Pages (auto-fills True Local + Whereis) → Hotfrog → Yelp → Facebook Page.
4. **Ongoing (gov/business-dev):** confirm ABN record → Tenders WA → AusTender → VendorPanel → check your council directory.

## Paste-ready assets (use verbatim for NAP consistency)

**Canonical NAP block** — reuse exactly on every profile:

```
Business name: C4 Studios
Area served:   Perth & Fremantle, Western Australia — serving clients Australia-wide (remote)
Email:         caleb@c4studios.com.au
Website:       https://c4studios.com.au
Founded:       2022
```
> Add your single chosen phone number before you start, and use it everywhere. If you adopt a street address, keep it identical across all listings (or run every profile as a service-area business with the address hidden).

**Business description — ~50 words:**
> C4 Studios is a Perth-based digital studio founded in 2022 by Caleb Scott. We design and build custom websites and web apps, deploy AI automation and AI agents, and produce brand photography, videography and motion graphics. We serve businesses across Perth, Fremantle and Western Australia, and remote clients Australia-wide.

**Business description — ~25 words:**
> C4 Studios is a Perth digital studio building custom websites, web apps, AI automation and AI agents, plus brand photography and video — serving WA and clients Australia-wide.

**Suggested categories:** Website designer / Web developer / Software company · Marketing agency / AI consultant · Photographer / Videographer.

> **Once your Google Business Profile and directory listings are live, note the ones that display public reviews** — those feed the star-rating rich snippets and AI "is this business good?" answers, and are worth a follow-up pass to wire `AggregateRating` schema into the site.
